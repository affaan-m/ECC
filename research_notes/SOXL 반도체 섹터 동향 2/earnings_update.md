## NVIDIA leadership and governance developments

### Takeaway
This overrides the prior note's framing. Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on Nov 12, and on Nov 23 the board unanimously reinstated him as President, CEO and director. NVDA closed up about 31% that day, a roughly $952B market-cap gain. The independent committee's investigation continues.

### Cited Findings
- 8-K and press release, Nov 23, 2026: Huang reinstated as President, CEO and director, effective immediately, by unanimous board vote after meetings with Huang. The board consulted senior US government officials and concluded his return poses no national security risk. Colette Kress served as interim CEO and remains CFO. — [NVIDIA 8-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)
- Investigation: an all-independent-director committee with independent counsel is probing reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. It will run to completion, and Huang says he supports it and will cooperate. — [NVIDIA 8-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)
- New governance steps: a National Security and Export Control Committee formed, and a search under way for a Chief Compliance Officer. Board Chair Harvey C. Jones: "We have every confidence in Jensen." — [NVIDIA 8-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)
- Market reaction Nov 23: NVDA up about 31.5% (intraday high about +36.6%), best day since 2003, about $952B added. Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq had their best day since April 2025. Figures vary by outlet (30-31.5%). — [Yahoo Finance live](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-november-23-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-141511975.html); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/markets/us-stocks-rebound-intl); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alisondurkee/2026/11/23/jensen-huang-returns-as-nvidia-ceo-despite-affairs-scandal-stock-surges-30/); [The Hill](https://thehill.com/business/6238473-nvidia-stock-surges-jensen-huang/)
- Background: the board opened the probe on Nov 8. Alice Huang (no relation) is described by lawmakers as a publicly reported Chinese intelligence asset, and Megaspeed reportedly bought more than $4.8B of Nvidia chips. These allegations are unconfirmed and come from lower-quality sites. — [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/nvidia-board-probe-jensen-huang-megaspeed-4-8-billion-2026/)
- A Nov 24 pullback was reported (headlines: "Nvidia stock falls after epic rally"; AFP: return "fails to dispel doubts"). The size was not verified. — [Search summary](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/markets/us-stocks-rebound-intl)

### Inferences
- The leveraged-ETF-relevant point is that a single-name 30% daily swing moved the whole sector. The prior note's CEO-status description (the board "does not plan to bring him back") is now superseded.
- The Nov 17 earnings release's "interim CEO" language is now obsolete.
