## 2. AI/data-center capex, HBM/memory cycle, demand drivers

### Takeaway
Hyperscaler 2026 capex is roughly $700B+ in aggregate with 2027 consensus estimates of about $1.1-1.2T (+29%), though official 2027 guidance was not found and Q3 updates were mixed (Microsoft trimmed, Alphabet held). Memory is in a historic upcycle with HBM contracted through 2027.

Microsoft reportedly trimmed its 2026 forecast to $170B from $175B. Totals roughly $720-745B. Aggregator-heavy and internally inconsistent; verify. — [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/big-tech-720-billion-data-center-spending-free-cash-flow-2026/); [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/big-tech/big-techs-ai-spending-plans-reach-725-billion)

- S&P-based 2027 estimates: Alphabet $357B, Amazon $319B, Meta $164B. — [Motley Fool](https://fool.com/investing/2026/09/06/hyperscalers-driving-ai-capex-cash-flow)

Autumn forecast expected in December. — [WSTS](https://www.wsts.org/esraCMS/extension/media/f/WST/7618/WSTS_FC-Release-2026-May.pdf); [Telecomlead](https://telecomlead.com/semiconductor/wsts-raises-semiconductor-forecast-market-to-reach-1-51-trillion-in-2026-grow-to-1-9-trillion-in-2027-126102)
