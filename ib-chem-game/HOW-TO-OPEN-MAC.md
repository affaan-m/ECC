# 🏰 How to Open ChemQuest on a Mac

**You do NOT need to know any coding. No installing. No internet.**
This is written for macOS 12.7.6. Just follow the pictures-in-words below.

---

## ✅ The 30-second way (try this first)

1. Open **Finder** (the blue smiley face in your Dock at the bottom of the screen).
2. Find the **`ib-chem-game`** folder and open it (double-click).
3. Inside, find the file called **`index.html`**.
   - Its icon usually looks like a **globe/compass** or the **Safari compass**.
4. **Double-click `index.html`.**
5. Your web browser opens and the game appears. **That's it — play!** 🎉

> The very first time you click anything in the game, the **medieval lute music**
> starts (browsers only allow sound after your first click — this is normal).

---

## 🟡 If double-clicking opens the WRONG app

Sometimes a Mac opens `.html` files in **TextEdit** or a code editor (you'd see a
wall of text instead of the game). Easy fix:

1. **Right-click** (or hold **Control** and click) on **`index.html`**.
2. In the menu, hover over **"Open With"**.
3. Choose a web browser: **Safari**, **Google Chrome**, **Firefox**, or **Edge**.
4. The game opens. 

**Want it to always open in the browser?**
1. Right-click `index.html` → **Get Info**.
2. In the window that appears, find **"Open with:"** and pick **Safari**.
3. Click **"Change All…"** and confirm. Done forever.

---

## 🔖 Make it easy to reopen later (optional)

Once the game is open in your browser:

- **Bookmark it:** press **⌘ + D** (Command and D), then **Add**. Next time, open
  it from your bookmarks.
- **Keep it handy:** drag the **`index.html`** file onto your **Desktop** so it's
  one double-click away. (Hold **⌥ Option** while dragging to copy it.)

---

## 🎵 ▶️ Turning things on inside the game

- **Music on/off:** click the little **🎵 note** button in the **bottom-left
  corner** at any time. (🔇 means it's muted.)
- **Type your answers instead of multiple choice:** click **🪶 Lab & Stats** on
  the home screen, then switch on **"Written recall"**. Now you type the answer
  and press **Enter**. Stuck? Tap **🗝 Hint**.
- **Relax / no timer:** in **Lab & Stats**, switch **"Question timer"** off.
- **Pick only your exam topics:** in **Lab & Stats → Exam scope**, switch off any
  topic that isn't on your exam.

Your level, gold and progress **save automatically** in the browser. As long as
you reopen the game in the **same browser** on the **same Mac**, it remembers you.

---

## 📱 Play on your phone too (optional)

The easiest way: use a free app like **Google Drive** or **Dropbox**.
1. On your Mac, upload the whole **`ib-chem-game`** folder to Drive/Dropbox.
2. On your phone, open the app, find the folder, and open **`index.html`**.
3. In Safari on iPhone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** to get an app icon.

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Page is blank or just shows text | You opened it in a text editor — see the **yellow section** above and "Open With → Safari". |
| No music | Click **anywhere** in the game once (browsers require a click first), and make sure the **🎵** corner button isn't on 🔇. |
| It looks plain / fonts look basic | You're offline so the fancy fonts didn't download — the game still works perfectly; it just uses your Mac's built-in fonts. |
| "Can't be opened because it is from an unidentified developer" | That message is for **apps**, not this file. This is a plain web page — just use **right-click → Open With → Safari**. |
| I want to start over | In **Lab & Stats**, scroll to **Danger zone → Reset**. |
